# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 13, 2026
**App:** The Next Covenant (iOS)
**Contact:** hallwren71@gmail.com

This policy explains what information The Next Covenant ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Summary

- Your plans, people, notes, and map points are stored **on your device** and, if you turn on sync, in **your own private iCloud account**. We cannot read them.
- We use **Firebase Authentication** (Google) to let you create an account and sign in with your email address and password.
- We do **not** sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

## Information we collect

### Account information
When you create an account, your **email address** and **password** are sent to Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google LLC. Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to us. Firebase also records basic account metadata (for example, account creation and last sign-in time). See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### Content you enter
The people, events, notes, achievements, and map locations you add are saved locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, this content is stored in the **private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account**, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not have access to your iCloud data.

### Location
With your permission, the app uses your device's location **only while the app is open** to show you on the map, pre-fill locations, and open directions in Apple Maps. Your location is not sent to us or stored on any server we operate. Locations you choose to save with events or people are stored as part of your content (see above).

### Face ID / passcode
If you enable app lock, Face ID or your device passcode is verified entirely by iOS. The app never receives your biometric data.

### Notifications
Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. We do not use push-notification servers to send you content.

### Backups
If you export a backup file, it is saved to a location you choose. We do not receive a copy.

## How we use information
We use your email address only to create and secure your account, let you sign in, and help you recover access. We do not use it for marketing.

## Sharing
We do not sell or rent your information. Data is processed only by the service providers described above (Google Firebase for sign-in, Apple iCloud for optional sync) to provide the app's features, or where required by law.

## Data retention and deletion
- You can delete your account from within the app's settings. This removes your Firebase Authentication account.
- You can delete synced data from iCloud in the app or in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
- Deleting the app removes data stored locally on your device.
- To request deletion or ask a question, contact hallwren71@gmail.com.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Security
Communication with Firebase and iCloud uses encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reputable providers and limit the data we collect.

## Changes
We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
