# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 13, 2026
**App:** The Next Covenant (iOS)
**Contact:** hallwren71@gmail.com

This policy explains what information The Next Covenant ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Summary

- Everything you enter (people, dates, notes, goals, and map pins) is stored **on your device** and synced through **your own private iCloud account**. We cannot see it.
- The app has **no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking**.
- We do **not** run any servers that receive your data, and we do not sell or share anything.

## Information the app stores

### Content you enter
The people, events, notes, lessons, commitments, milestones, goals, photos, and places you add are saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, this content syncs to the **private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account** so it appears on your other devices. That data is governed by Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We have no access to your iCloud data.

### Settings
Your name, title, mission city, and app preferences are stored on your device only.

### Location
With your permission, the app uses your device's location **only while the app is open**, to center the map and help you place pins. Your location is not sent to us or stored on any server we operate. Pins you choose to save are stored as part of your content (see above).

### Photos
If you choose a photo for someone in your Area Book, only that photo is saved, as part of your content. The app doesn't access the rest of your photo library.

### Face ID / passcode
If you turn on app lock, Face ID or your device passcode is checked entirely by iOS. The app never receives biometric data.

## Sharing
We don't collect your information, so there's nothing to sell, rent, or share. Apple processes your synced data only to provide iCloud.

## Deleting your data
- **Settings → Erase Everything** in the app deletes all of your content from the app and from your iCloud.
- You can also manage the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
- Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

## Changes
We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
